Evaluating Inference-Time Defences Against Jailbreak Attacks

Adam Kaegi  /  Ryan Maxin  /  Vishesh Gupta  /  Varun Mathur

Ten defence configurations, four jailbreak attack families, one fixed target model, 2,500 evaluated responses. The study asks whether inference-time defences generalise beyond the attacks they were built for, whether stacking them helps, and what the protection costs in refused legitimate requests, latency, and model calls.

Defences do not generalise

Layering pays, then stops

The full stack reached 1.0% attack success against the leaner stack's 1.5% — one additional attack stopped, for five times the model calls.

Benign requests refused

Protection has a price

Stronger defence configurations refused far more legitimate requests and added roughly 11 seconds of mean response latency. Minimising attack success alone picks the wrong system.
